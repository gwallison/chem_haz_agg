# GHS Hazard Classification: Complete Health and Environmental Table

## The Tiering Rule

This document classifies all GHS Health (H3xx) and Environmental (H4xx) hazard codes into two tiers:

* **Tier 1 (Known Serious Hazard):** Assigned if the hazard Category is **1 or 2**, OR if the Signal Word is **"Danger"**.
* **Tier 2 (Less Serious Hazard):** All other classified hazards.
