*[ATSDR]: Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry (US)
*[CAMEO]: A database of hazardous chemical datasheets that emergency responders and planners can use to get response recommendations and predict hazards. Developed by NOAA and EPA (US)
*[CAS]: Chemical Abstracts Service
*[CASRN]: CAS Registration Number, a standard, authoritative chemical ID
*[CMR]: Carcinogenicity, Mutagenicity and Reproductive hazards: a grouping of GHS hazard classes
*[E&H Chemical Class]: Elsner & Hoelzer (2016) classification scheme
*[ECHA]: European Chemicals Agengy (EU)
*[EHP]: Environmental Health Project is "a nonprofit public health organization that defends public health in the face of shale gas development."
*[EPA]: Environmental Protection Agency (US)
*[GHS]: Global Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals, a United Nations standard for consistent chemical hazard communication worldwide
*[IRIS]: Integrated Risk Information System (US EPA)
*[NIOSH]: National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health; CDC (US)
*[NJ]: New Jersey (US)
*[OECD]: "International organization that works to establish evidence-base international standards," including work on the "Existing Chemical Database"
*[Tier 1]: (red) KNOWN HAZARDS: Based on the official GHS. These chemicals have internationally recognized, significant hazards like carcinogenicity, reproductive toxicity, or acute toxicity.
*[Tier 2]: (orange) EXPANDED PERSPECTIVE: Goes beyond the slow-moving GHS to include more current data from scientific literature, other regulatory agencies, and predictive models. This tier provides a more complete view of potential hazards when the GHS has not formally made conclusions.
*[Tier 3]: (blue) DEMONSTRATED LOW OR MODERATE HAZARD: This is reserved for chemicals without Tier 1 or 2 indicators, but direct studies support a favorable safety profile for a given hazard class.
*[Tier 4]: (gray) :DATA DEFICIENT: Indicates there is not enough information to make a confident hazard assessment. An absence of data does not mean a chemical is safe; it means its risk is unknown and a precautionary approach is warranted
*[UVCB]: Unknown, Variable, Complex reaction product, or Biological material.  A TCSA designation for materials that are complex and often difficult to define chemically
